# Code of Conduct

## Our promise

We want every contributor and visitor to have a good experience. In exchange, we ask you to be excellent to each other.

We pledge to act in a way that is welcoming to everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

**Positive behavior** includes: showing empathy, being respectful of different opinions and viewpoints, giving and accepting feedback graciously, taking responsibility, apologizing when needed, and focusing on what is best for the project and community.

**Unacceptable behavior** includes: sexualized language or imagery, trolling or insult/derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, public or private harassment, publishing others' private information (doxxing) without explicit permission, and any conduct a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to [meetcode99@gmail.com](mailto:meetcode99@gmail.com). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The maintainer reserves the right to remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and to ban (temporarily or permanently) any contributor for behaviors deemed inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces (repo, issues, discussions, shared docs) and whenever a person is representing the project (for example, posting scan results in public).

Past adoption is based on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), v2.1.
