# Support

Thank you for using RepoGrade!

- Question about a grade or a check? Open a [Discussion](https://github.com/fastpresspages/repograde/discussions) and include the repo, your scan output and what you expected.
- Something is broken? [Open a bug issue](https://github.com/fastpresspages/repograde/issues/new?template=bug.yml) with the check id from `repograde --list`.
- Need something deeper or stuck with a real deadline? Read our [docs](https://fastpresspages.github.io/repograde/), request a [free human review](https://fastpresspages.github.io/repograde/#contact), or email [meetcode99@gmail.com](mailto:meetcode99@gmail.com).
- Security issue only: follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) privately.

We aim to read everything within 48 hours. Nothing in support is paid: paid optional extras (deep audits, fix sprints) are described honestly at [FastPress Labs](https://github.com/fastpresspages#readme), and there is always free help.

No fake metrics, no star-bait. Thanks for building in public!
